# Change Log: 2026-08-18 03:07:15

## Summary of Modifications

1. **Fixed WhatsApp QR Interception & Modal Rendering**:
   - Fixed `GET /api/whatsapp/status` to accurately report `connected: bool` strictly when `is_logged_in` is `True`, ensuring that active raw sockets without authentication do not prematurely report as "connected".
   - Added `qr_code` and `qr_ready` verification to `/api/whatsapp/status`.
   - Updated `GET /api/whatsapp/qr_image` to include `Cache-Control: no-cache, no-store, must-revalidate` response headers so browser clients always receive the latest QR code image upon generation.
   - Updated `frontend/index.html` `checkWhatsAppStatus()` to display the QR image whenever `data.qr_ready` or `data.qr_code` is present and `!data.logged_in`.
